About

I work on few-body quantum systems driven by external fields. Most of my research is on helium, where electron correlation creates doubly excited states that one-electron pictures cannot explain.

I write and validate my own codes, mainly in Fortran and Python, and develop & validate analytic and semi-classical theories against full numerical solutions of the Schrödinger equation.
